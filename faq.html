Commission process

Revisions

All commissions include one revision round. A revision means adjustments to the existing concept — changes to colors, text, layout, or minor compositional tweaks. Additional revision rounds can be purchased if needed.
A revision adjusts something within the agreed scope — tweaking a color palette, repositioning an element, or refining typography. A new project is a complete change of direction, concept, or style that differs significantly from the original brief. If you're unsure, just ask — I'll always be upfront about what falls into which category.

Commission policy.

Last updated — May 2025

Commission terms

By submitting a commission request and paying the deposit, you agree to these terms. Please read them carefully before proceeding.

  • All commissions begin with a submitted brief and mutual agreement on scope, price, and timeline.
  • Work does not begin until the 50% deposit has been received.
  • BoviBerry reserves the right to decline any commission request for any reason.
  • Commissions are taken on a first-come, first-served basis. Being added to a waitlist does not guarantee a spot.
  • Any changes to the project scope after work has begun may result in additional charges.
Important: Submitting a commission request does not constitute a binding agreement. An agreement is only formed once the quote is accepted and the deposit is paid.

Quotes & scope

Prices listed on the pricing page are starting points for a standard project of that type, not fixed final totals.

  • If a request goes beyond the typical scope of the listed piece — additional concepts, unusual complexity, extra deliverables, or requirements that meaningfully expand the work — the quote may exceed the listed price.
  • Any amount above the listed price is explained in writing, itemized, and agreed to before work begins.
  • You will never be charged more than the quote you accepted. No surprise invoices, ever.
  • If you request changes in direction or additional deliverables after work has started, the additional work is re-quoted separately and nothing proceeds until you approve it.
  • A quote is valid for 14 days from the date it's sent. After that it may be re-issued at current availability and rates.
In plain terms: the listed price is what a typical version of that project costs. If yours is bigger or more involved than typical, the quote reflects that — and you'll always see the number and agree to it before anything starts.

Subscription terms

These terms apply to the Botanical Harvest (streaming) and Farmers Market (social media) monthly plans.

  • Subscriptions bill monthly on the date you start and renew automatically until cancelled.
  • Cancel any time with 14 days notice before your next billing date. There is no long-term contract and no cancellation fee on subscriptions.
  • The current month is not prorated or refunded once billed — you keep access and deliverables through the end of that cycle.
  • Unused monthly deliverables (flyers, story templates, etc.) do not roll over to the following month.
  • Paid ad spend, logo or brand identity design, video editing, and third-party platform costs are not included and are quoted separately.
  • Account access needed for audience engagement is handled through official tools or delegated access wherever possible. Shared passwords are avoided.

Payment & refunds

All prices are in USD. Payment is split into two parts to protect both parties.

  • 50% deposit is due before work begins. This is non-refundable once work has started.
  • Remaining 50% is due upon delivery, before final files are released.
  • If a project is cancelled by the client after work has begun, the deposit is non-refundable and 25% of the remaining balance is due. This covers the time reserved and work already completed.
  • If a project is cancelled by BoviBerry before work begins, a full refund of any payments made will be issued.
  • No refunds are issued once final files have been delivered.
  • Late payment of the final balance may result in a delay in file delivery.
Refund window: If you are unsatisfied with the final delivery and the revision round has not been used, please reach out within 3 days of delivery to discuss. Refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis and are not guaranteed.

Revision policy

One revision round is included with every commission. Here's exactly what that means:

  • A revision round covers reasonable adjustments within the original agreed scope — colors, text, layout, and minor compositional changes.
  • Revisions must be requested within 7 days of delivery. After this window, the project is considered complete.
  • Additional full revision rounds beyond the included one are $40 each. Larger or more complex projects may be quoted individually.
  • A complete redesign or major change of direction after delivery constitutes a new project and will be quoted separately.
  • Revision requests should be consolidated into one clear list — not sent piece by piece over multiple messages.

Copyright & usage rights

Understanding who owns what after a commission is important. Here's how it works:

  • BoviBerry retains full copyright and ownership of all original artwork and designs created.
  • Upon full payment, clients receive a personal use license — meaning you can use the work for personal, non-commercial purposes.
  • For commercial use — including selling merchandise, advertising, business branding, or monetized content — a commercial license must be purchased. This is quoted separately.
  • You may not resell, redistribute, or claim authorship of any work created by BoviBerry.
  • BoviBerry reserves the right to display all completed work in the portfolio unless a confidentiality agreement is arranged beforehand.
  • Crediting BoviBerry when sharing work publicly is appreciated but not required for personal use.
NFTs & AI training: Work created by BoviBerry may not be minted as NFTs or used to train AI models without explicit written permission.

Turnaround times

Estimated timelines begin from the date the deposit is received, not the date the commission is submitted.

  • Graphic design pieces — 3 to 5 business days
  • Photo manipulation — 2 to 4 business days
  • YouTube Starter Kit — 5 to 7 business days
  • Creator Pro Kit — 7 to 10 business days
  • Brand Identity Kit — 10 to 14 business days
  • Custom / complex projects — timeline agreed upon individually

These are estimates, not guarantees. Delays may occur if client feedback or revision requests take longer than expected. Rush delivery may be available for an additional fee — inquire in your brief.

Delays caused by the client — such as late payment, slow feedback, or unclear briefs — will not count against the delivery timeline. The clock pauses until the required information or payment is received.
