Before you book

How pricing works.

A few things worth knowing so your quote never comes as a surprise.

Listed prices are starting points. The prices on this page reflect a standard project of that type. If your request goes beyond the typical scope of that piece — additional concepts, unusual complexity, extra deliverables, or requirements that meaningfully expand the work — your quote may come in higher than the listed price. Any difference is always explained and agreed in writing before work begins. You will never be charged more than the quote you accepted.

Payment structure. One-off projects are split 50/50 — a 50% deposit secures your spot and starts the work, with the remaining 50% due on delivery before final files are released. Subscriptions are billed in full at the start of each monthly cycle.

Scope changes mid-project. If you decide to change direction or add deliverables after work has started, that's completely fine — it just gets re-quoted for the additional work. Nothing moves forward until you approve the revised amount.

All prices are in USD. Full details on refunds, revisions, turnaround times, and copyright live on the FAQ & Policy page.
